**Retro eXo** is the name given to a collection of projects focused on historical (or retro!) computing platforms. Encompassing everything from a collection of Windows 3.xx games to a lovingly curated selection of demos taken straight from the demoscene world, the eXo projects are maintained by a dedicated group of volunteers who have put thousands of hours into the work, all in the aim of preservation for future generations. 

This repository contains a selection of reviews as part of my writing portfolio.
